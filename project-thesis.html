Sustainable supply chains under environmental policy stringency

University of Basel · energy-intensive SMEs · thesis submitted

  • R
  • Excel
  • Multilevel models
  • Logistic regression
  • Policy analysis
Question
How does stricter environmental policy affect whether energy-intensive SMEs implement sustainable supply chain practices?
What I did
Estimated multilevel models with clustered errors in R and Excel, and tested how digital technology and institutional quality change the effect.
Outcome
Linked eco-design to institutional quality and energy import dependence using logistic regression, reported as average marginal effects and turned into clear findings.

Highlights

  • Multilevel models with clustered standard errors.
  • Digital technology and institutional quality tested as conditions on the policy effect.
  • Logistic regression on eco-design, reported as average marginal effects.
