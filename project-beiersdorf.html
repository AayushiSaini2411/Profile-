Beiersdorf HR Analytics: piloting a four-day work week

HR Analytics colloquium, University of Basel · project proposal, team of three

  • KPI design
  • Dashboard
  • Difference-in-differences
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • GDPR
Question
Would a four-day work week keep productivity and improve wellbeing at Beiersdorf's Hamburg headquarters, and how can that be tested before a wider rollout?
What I did
Planned a six-month controlled pilot on the 100–80–100 model (full pay, 80% time, same output) with the team: scope, a 24-week project timeline and an €85–120k budget. Built the KPI framework and a seven-module dashboard, and used difference-in-differences to measure impact against a matched comparison group.
Outcome
A structured rollout plan with stakeholders and risks mapped, GDPR safeguards included, and assumptions and mitigations documented.

Highlights

  • Pilot functions: Marketing, Finance, HR, Digital and Procurement at the Hamburg headquarters.
  • Pilot group measured against a matched comparison group.
  • KPIs in three groups: business, people and process.
  • Evaluation combines surveys, KPI data and interviews.
  • Budget of €85–120k across a 24-week project timeline.
