Sep 2024 – Present

Business and Sales AssistantAggarwal AG · Basel

+

My longest role so far, held alongside my master's: the day-to-day link between B2B customers and the suppliers behind their orders, backed by forecasting and reporting for management.

1What I did

  • Act as the main contact for 20 to 30 B2B accounts from quote to delivery, and stay with queries and complaints until they are solved.
  • Compare and negotiate supplier quotes on quantity, quality, delivery time and price, then follow up quotations, lead times and deliveries.
  • Select and evaluate suppliers across continents on sustainability (packaging, logistics, transport) alongside price.
  • Run EU product compliance checks on labelling, ingredient and allergen information, importer details and supplier documents.
  • Forecast demand from past sales in SAP APO and keep supplier and store data accurate.
  • Simulate options for distribution centres and stock levels, calculate safety stocks, and time orders for seasonal peaks and lows, so perishable products are neither overstocked nor spoiled.
  • Manage allocation and reorders so the right products are available at the right time.
  • Manage the snacks category of 40 to 45 products, within a company range of about 4,000 products from 60 suppliers.
  • Build Power BI dashboards for management: revenue and sales per article category, where the gaps lie and what to improve.

2Tools I used

SAP APOOMPPower BIERP systemPointSales

3What I learned

  • How to weigh price against quality, delivery time and sustainability when choosing a supplier.
  • To read product documentation critically: labels, ingredients, allergens and importer details.
  • To stay calm and factual with a customer until the problem is actually solved.
  • To pair the numbers with time on the ground: a dashboard shows where the gap is, customers and the market show why.

4Where I'll use it

In a procurement, supply chain or operations role I can take on supplier and customer communication from the first week, forecast and report with the tools already in use, and I bring the habit of checking the details before anything ships.
