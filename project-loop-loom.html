Loop & Loom: circular fashion for Gen Z

Startup Academy Switzerland · Team Lead

  • Team leadership
  • Customer testing
  • Pricing
  • Financial model
Question
Will Gen Z pay for sustainable, circular fashion, and at what price?
What I did
Led a team of five. Defined target segments, ran customer testing, designed a concept store combining recycled fashion, customisation workshops and a coffee bar, and built an Excel financial model with CHF 55–60k start-up capital and CHF 128k Year 1 costs.
Outcome
Sustainability mattered, but price came first. Offers were priced from CHF 15 to 120 and checked for viability against the model. The team finished 2nd out of 20 teams in the final pitch.

Highlights

  • Finished 2nd out of 20 teams, after reworking the product several times in response to criticism.
  • Target customers: Gen Z and millennials aged 16–35, schools and local ethical consumers.
  • Year 1 revenue mix: workshops 62.8%, fashion sales 21.9%, coffee shop 13.9%, customisation 1.5%.
  • Start-up capital of CHF 55–60k from founders, an impact investor and public or culture grants.
  • Year 1 costs of CHF 128k: rent 60k, staffing 40k, materials 15k, marketing and café 13k.
